# chatgpt plus subscription: what $20 a month actually buys, and how to pay when your card keeps getting declined

There are two separate problems hiding inside the phrase "ChatGPT Plus subscription," and most people hit them in order. The first is deciding whether $20 a month is worth it. The second, sneakier one arrives at checkout: your card gets declined, the payment page spins, and suddenly you're reading forum threads about virtual cards at 1am. This piece handles both. The first half is OpenAI's own plan structure. The second half is the practical question of getting the money from your bank to OpenAI when the normal route doesn't work.

## What a ChatGPT Plus subscription actually is

Plus is OpenAI's individual paid plan at **$20 per month, billed monthly**. Two details from OpenAI's own help centre that people regularly get wrong:

- There is no annual plan and no multi-month prepayment option. You pay monthly, full stop.
- API usage is **not** included. If you're building something on the API, that's a separate bill with its own pricing.

In practice, Plus covers the ChatGPT website and the supported apps, so one subscription serves your browser, your desktop client and your phone as long as you're signed into the same account. That matters later, because it's the reason a subscription service that upgrades *your existing account* is different from one that hands you a stranger's login.

## Where Plus sits among the official tiers

What Plus buys over Free is mostly model access and tooling rather than raw message count: the newest model family, higher caps on images, files and voice, Deep Research, Agent mode, Sora, and no ads. Exact numerical limits move too often to commit to memory — one independent pricing tracker currently lists Plus at roughly 160 messages per three hours and about 10 full Deep Research runs plus 15 lighter ones per month. Treat any specific number you read, including that one, as a snapshot rather than a spec.

## The part the pricing pages skip: paying for it

OpenAI's checkout is picky in ways that have nothing to do with whether you have money. The recurring failures:

- Cards issued in mainland China, and most cards from other regions OpenAI's processor treats as high risk
- Prepaid virtual cards from issuers OpenAI doesn't recognise
- Cards whose billing country doesn't match the region you're browsing from
- Newly created cards with no transaction history

If none of that applies to you, stop here — subscribe directly, and you're done. The rest of this article is for the people who've already watched a perfectly valid card get rejected three times.

The workarounds people actually use, and what each one costs you:

| Route | How it works | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your own card | Fastest, cheapest, cleanest | Often simply declined depending on where the card is issued |
| App Store gift cards | Buy a US-region gift card, load a US Apple ID, subscribe through the iOS app | Needs a US Apple ID and a reliable gift-card seller; works, but there's setup involved |
| Virtual cards | Top up a prepaid card, use it at OpenAI's checkout | Issuer risk, top-up fees, and cards that die mid-subscription. WildCard, the best-known option, ran into trouble in 2024 and its team relaunched under a different brand |
| Shared or pooled accounts | Several people use one Plus account | Cheapest per month, but it isn't your account, your chat history isn't private, and context disappears when the operator rotates accounts |
| Third-party subscription services | You pay locally; they complete the charge on your own account | Costs meaningfully more than $20 and requires trusting a middleman with temporary access |

That last row is where BeWild comes in, and it's worth being precise about what it is and isn't.

## The upgrade, step by step

The flow is short, but the order of operations is where people get stuck.

1. Register on the platform and open the ChatGPT Plus section. Arriving through the invite link applies the code.
2. Pick your duration. One month if you're testing, three months if you've decided — the per-month cost drops noticeably on the longer packs.
3. Log into **your own** ChatGPT account in the same browser you're using for the order. This is the step that quietly breaks things if you're in incognito mode or signed into the wrong account.
4. Follow the provided link and copy the code or session value it returns, then paste it back into the platform.
5. Confirm the email address on the ChatGPT account. Read it carefully — this is the last checkpoint before money moves.
6. Pay by QR code and wait. Ten to thirty minutes is the normal window.

Afterwards, refresh ChatGPT and check that Plus features are active. If they aren't within an hour or so, that's a support ticket, not a waiting game.

## Where it goes wrong

This is the section promotional write-ups skip, and it's the one that should shape your decision.

**Activation and renewal failures are documented.** In August 2026, one user's log records a three-month Plus plan that didn't auto-renew on schedule, plus a separate two-month order that showed as completed while the ChatGPT account stayed on the free tier. Both ended in refunds, but only after contact with support. Renewal reliability has been the weakest part of the experience, and a subscription that lapses silently is worse than one that fails loudly.

**Accounts getting cut off is a real, recurring theme.** On the Linux.do forum, multiple users reported Plus and Pro accounts dropping — including via residential-IP setups — with BeWild named among the services involved. Some users reported getting replacements under the warranty they'd bought; others were still waiting. That's a forum thread, not a controlled study, and it's full of secondhand claims. It's also not something you can dismiss if you're about to prepay three months.

**Session refreshes get requested.** The platform has asked users to re-upload a fresh session when an existing subscription looked at risk, so it can complete remaining months. Reasonable operationally. It also means the temporary access you granted isn't always as temporary as you assumed.

**Self-referral is banned.** BeWild's rewards and affiliate policy — in force since 27 April 2026 — prohibits inviting yourself through alternative accounts, payment methods, devices or identities, and reserves the right to cancel rewards or terminate accounts. Users have reported invite codes still applying at checkout while doing exactly that. Checkout accepting something is not the same as the policy allowing it.

None of this means the service doesn't work. Plenty of orders complete normally, and refund cases did get resolved. It means you're buying convenience from a third party, and the premium you pay doesn't come with an OpenAI-backed guarantee.

## Common questions

**Does Plus work across web, desktop and mobile?** Yes, one subscription on one account covers all of them. A shared-pool account works too, but you inherit whatever session state the operator leaves you.

**Can I cancel?** If you bought through a third-party service, cancellation lives in that service's dashboard, not in ChatGPT's billing settings. Before your cycle ends, log in and confirm whether renewal is on and what it will charge — that's the specific failure mode documented above.

**Does this break OpenAI's terms?** Nobody can hand you a clean yes. OpenAI's terms put payment accuracy and account security on you, and the session-token model means a third party holds temporary access to your account. That's the trade you're making, and it's why the "keep your own account" distinction matters so much: the risk profile is very different from buying a shared login, but it isn't zero.

**What if I just want the cheapest possible access?** The shared pool at under $10 a month is the cheapest route that isn't asking you to do anything technical. Go in knowing the chat history isn't private and the account isn't yours.
